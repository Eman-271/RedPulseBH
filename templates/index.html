Process

How RedPulseBH Works

1. Register as a Donor

Create your profile so hospitals can identify your blood type and eligibility quickly.

2. Hospitals Submit Requests

Authorized hospitals post urgent or scheduled requests for specific blood types.

3. Donors Respond & Donate

Matching donors are notified and can coordinate their donation with the hospital.
